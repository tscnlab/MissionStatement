# Mission statement change history

Change summaries added: 25 September 2026.

The dates below are the original revision dates recorded in the snapshots.
Each entry describes changes from the preceding version; v1.0 records the
initial statement. These retrospective summaries do not alter the snapshots.

## v1.5 | 10 September 2026

### Introduction

- Recorded the Milan lab retreat on 10 September 2026 in the statement's history.

### Section order

- Reordered the six principles by priority: inclusive lab culture, meaningful and societally relevant science, wellbeing, open scholarship, reproducibility, and sustainability.

### Commitment to meaningful and societally relevant science

- Added the “Nachtmensch oder Frühaufsteher?” roadshow, with a website link, as an example of public engagement through interactive experiments and dialogue with scientists.

### Wellbeing and work-life balance

- Acknowledged that the lab has faced wellbeing and work-life-balance challenges throughout its lifetime.
- Identified recognising and acknowledging these challenges as the crucial first step towards addressing them.
- Added open conversations about workload, stress and support needs, followed by practical changes.
- Added chronotype-friendly working arrangements and consideration of individual sleep and activity timing when scheduling meetings and shared activities.
- Separated the text on recognising challenges, working arrangements, and time away from work into distinct paragraphs.

### Open scholarship and open science

- Replaced the general aim to normalise open science with a commitment to public GitHub repositories containing version-controlled analysis scripts, documentation and reuse instructions.
- Expanded data sharing into documented datasets and standalone data releases, including releases not tied to individual publications; consolidated the earlier data-sharing sentence into this commitment.
- Changed encouraging and wanting to aid replication attempts to encouraging and supporting them.
- Explicitly recognised the time, effort and funding needed for replication, and linked these needs to planning replication work and discussing the support the lab can provide.
- Gave replication and support requests their own paragraph.

### Reproducibility and scientific integrity

- Added independent computational reproducibility checks, such as CODECHECK where appropriate, to document whether others can run the supplied code and data and reproduce the outputs.
- Removed the sentence about collecting pseudonymised or anonymised data whenever possible.

### Sustainability

- Added conscious and intentional resource use, including considering whether resources are needed, can be shared or reused, and their environmental impact.
- Removed the target of one sustainability-related topic in every five student projects.
- Replaced the long-term teaching aspiration with a commitment to integrate sustainability into teaching and consider relevant research questions within the field.

### Version history

- Updated the version and date to v1.5, 10 September 2026.

## v1.4 | 2 September 2024

### Introduction

- Recorded the 2024 lab retreat at Schneefernerhaus on the Zugspitze.

### Inclusive, diverse and collaborative lab culture

- Added critical reflection on the lab's ability to uphold an inclusive environment and a commitment to seek training.
- Changed participation in institutional access programmes to contributing to them, adding linked examples of CaCTüS and Girls’Day.
- Removed virtual lab visits from the examples of widening access.
- Changed the neurodiversity and active-bystander links from linked phrases to asterisk reference links.

### Open scholarship and open science

- Explicitly extended the open-scholarship commitment to research and teaching; removed education from the subsequent list of scientific-process examples and separately emphasised preregistration and publishing.
- Replaced routinely sharing data with sharing data as research outputs.
- Corrected spacing around the scientific-talk recordings link and shortened “in order to make” to “to make”.

### Reproducibility and scientific integrity

- Separated the discussion of researcher degrees of freedom and research integrity into a new paragraph; the wording was unchanged.

### Sustainability

- Reframed sustainability in teaching and the target of one sustainability-related student project in five as long-term intentions.
- Changed the aim to evaluate and review the carbon footprint of activities to a statement that the lab considers it.
- Replaced the ellipsis character in the waste examples with three periods.

### Commitment to meaningful and societally relevant science

- Added a commitment to seek and create opportunities to apply and translate the lab's work, with the Ladenburg White Paper as a linked example.
- Added a link to the lab's podcasts and adjusted punctuation in the public-engagement examples.
- Shortened “driven by a combination of curiosity and the wish for discovery” to “driven by curiosity and the wish for discovery”.
- Split the text into separate paragraphs covering scientific purpose, public engagement and application, and accessibility.

### Wellbeing and work-life balance

- Replaced the opening assertion that the lab prioritises wellbeing with an acknowledgement that academia threatens wellbeing and work-life balance and that addressing this is a major challenge.
- Added a commitment to work without losing sight of wellbeing.
- Removed the statement that time management implements work-life balance by preventing overwork.
- Changed employees choosing their own working schedules to designing their own working schedules.

### Version history

- Updated the version and date to v1.4, 2 September 2024.
